God of War: Ragnarok


About

God of War Ragnorak is an action adventure game developed by Santa Monica Studio and released in 2022.

With God of War Ragnarok, players join Kratos, our main character, and Atreus on a mythic journey for answers before Ragnarok arrives. Together, the father and son duo risk it all as they journey to each of the Nine Realms and face off against challenging foes in brutal combats.

Reviews


LEVEL (Czech Republic)


" I can't help but tip my hat to the entire Santa Monica team. The deeper you delve into the game, the more you'll be drawn into the stunning story of a father and son against the backdrop of perhaps history's most horrific war. Throw in some casually endearing humour, great and fluid gameplay, and wrap it all up with an amazing audiovisual experience. "



Gameblog.fr

Forbes
