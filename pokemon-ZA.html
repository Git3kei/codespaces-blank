Pokemon Legends: Z-A


About

Pokemon Legends: Z-A is an action role-playing game developed by Game Freak and released in 2025.


Pokemon Legends: Z-A takes place inside Lumiose city of Kalos Region, exclusively, and follows the events that took place 5 years ago in Pokemon X and Y.
