Top 5 Action Games




1. God of War: Ragnarok

God of War Ragnorak is an action adventure game developed by Santa Monica Studio and released in 2022.


With God of War Ragnarok, players join Kratos, our main character, and Atreus on a mythic journey for answers before Ragnarok arrives. Together, the father and son duo risk it all as they journey to each of the Nine Realms and face off against challenging foes in brutal combats.



2. Devil May Cry 5

Devil May Cry 5 is an action-adventure game developed by Capcom and released in 2019.

The setting for Devil May Cry 5 takes place after the events of Devil May Cry 4, whereby we play as Nero who fights off the widespread demon invasion.



3. Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice is an action-adventure game developed by FromSoftware and released in 2019.


In Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice you play as the'one-armed wolf', a disgraced and disfigured warrior rescued from the brink of death.



4. Bayonette 2

Bayonetta 2 is an action-adventure game developed by PlatinumGames and released in 2014.


Bayonetta 2 revolves around Bayonetta having to rescue her friend Jeanne's soul, who was dragged to inferno, as well as Loki, an amnesiac boy.



5. Assassin's Creed Valhalla

Assassin's Creed Valhalla is an action role-playing game developed by Ubisoft Montreal and released in 2020.


Assassin's Creed Valhalla is set in the 9th-century CE Norway and England, following Eivor Varinsdottir, a Viking raider who leaves a war-torn Norway to build a new home for the Ravenclan.
