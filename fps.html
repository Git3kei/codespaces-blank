Top 5 FPS Games




1. Valorant

Valorant is a character-based 5v5 tactical shooter set on the global stage.


Valorant follows the perspective of the VALORANT Protocol agents of Alpha Earth, a near-future Earth impacted by large advancements in technology, a mysterious global event known as First Light.



2. Counter Strike: Global Offensive

Counter Strike: Global Offensive expands upon the team-based action gameplay that it pioneered when it was launched over 2 decades ago.


Counter Strike: Global Offensive pits two teams, Terrorists and Counter-Terrorists, against each other in different objective-based game modes.



3. Apex Legends

Apex Legends is a battle royale-hero shooter game developed by Respawn Entertainment and released in 2019.


Apex Legends is set in a sci-fi universe decades after the conclusion of the Frontier War between the Interstellar Manufacturing Corporation and the Frontier Militia.



4. Call of Duty: Black OPS 7

Call of Duty: Black OPS 7 is a first-person shooter co-developed by Treyarch and Raven Software and released in 2025.


Call of Duty: Black OPS 7 follows a JSOC unit led by David Mason as they investigate the apparent return of deceased terrorist Raul Mendez.



5. Overwatch

Overwatch is a live service multiplayer first-person shooter developed by Blizzard Entertainment in 2016.


Overwatch takes place on a near-future earth dealing with global conflicts between humanity, rogue robots called Omnics, and opposing factions.
