Bayonetta 2


About

Bayonetta 2 is an action-adventure game developed by PlatinumGames and released in 2014.


Bayonetta 2 revolves around Bayonetta having to rescue her friend Jeanne's soul, who was dragged to inferno, as well as Loki, an amnesiac boy.
