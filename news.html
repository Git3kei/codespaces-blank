  2. What streamers to watch gameplays of


    Who says one must comsume video games simply by playing them. With the advancements made in the online sphere of streaming videos, many people have found the joys of watching other people livetsream their playthroughs onto platforms like Youtube, Twitch, and the likes of such.

    As of 2026 we now have more than enough gaming channels to watch and enjoy while feeling like we're actively playing a videogame, or maybe we enjoy playing such videos just to immerse ourselves in the stories of these videogames without the need to play ourselves. Here are some top recommended streamers, including not only fleshtubers, but Vtubers alike:



    CaseOh

    CaseOh is an American streamer best known for streaming gameplays and reaction videos on a variety of platforms like Youtube, Twitch, and Tiktok. His most watched videos are his horror video game plays.


    Kenji

    Sun Kenji, is an American VTuber who streams games and reaction videos on Twitch while posting VODs onto hsi YouTube. He quickly built a strong fanbase through his comedic and warm personality, which makes his videos a great watch if you're looking to have a great laugh or two.


    Pokimane

    Pokimane, or Imane Anys, is a Moroccan and Canadian streamer who started off as a video game streamer and was well known to be one of the first few female streamers. She livestreams on Twitch, playing some notable games like, Valorant and Fortnite, but has since branched out to other genres of games, while also streaming 'just chatting' videos.


    Ironmouse

    Ironmouse is a Puerto Rican VTuber who streams on Twitch, and is considered a veteran, having started streaming in August of 2017. Her content consists of streaming video games and karaoke sessions. Her high-pitched voice and sassy personality had brought many fans to adore her, and gave her enough of a reach to inspire fellow sufferers of CVID, a rare immune disorder.


    TommyInnit

    TommyInnit, or Thomas Michael Simons, is a Youtuber and Twitch streamer, who gained popularity over his more popular content, Minecraft streams. He has a fun personality that make many kids avid viewers of his videos.


    Dokibird

    Dokibird is an indie VTuber who had originally retired from streaming in May of 2021, but has made her return since February of 2024. Streaming mainly on Youtube, Dokibird livestreams mainly FPS games like Apex Legends, while occasionally taking part in ESport competitions.
