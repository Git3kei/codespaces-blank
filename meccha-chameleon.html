Meccha Chameleon


About

Meccha Chameleon is a viral multiplayer party game that has taken the gaming world by storm since releasing in 2026.


Meccha Chameleon is a multiplayer where players are split into two teams during a match, chameleons (hiders) and hunter (seekers). It's closely akin to hide-and-seek but with painting oneslef.

Game8


" Meccha Chameleon feels like a “forever game” in a sea of party games on Steam, and part of its appeal is all thanks to its inventive twist on the hide-and-seek genre.
