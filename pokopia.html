Pokopia


About

Pokopia is a life simulation game co-developed by Game Freak and Koei Tecmo's Omega Force and released in 2026.


Pokopia is a cozy life simulation that allows for players to play as a Ditto in human form. The gameplay consists of crafting buildings and decorations while growing a community of Pokemon to live alongside.
