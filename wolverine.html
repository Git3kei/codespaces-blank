Wolverine


About

Wolverine is an action-adventure game developed by Insomniac Games and released in 2026.


Wolverine follows the narrative of an amnesiac mutant, wolverine, who rejoins his former unit, Team X, to investigate the abduction of displaced mutants by industrialist Bolivar Trask and the Reavers militia, while searching for answers regarding his own past.

Reviews


Impulsegamer


" Marvel’s Wolverine… what a ride! Insomniac has successfully honoured the core of Wolverine and translated it into one of the best games of 2026. From the very first moments of the game, you are taken on a jaw-dropping and adrenaline-pumping adventure as you slice and dice your way through an army of enemies as you try to save the world from those seeking to control it. From its flawless gameplay to its realistic graphics, Marvel’s Wolverine undeniably boasts one of the best narratives of the year, which is enhanced by its stellar cast who successfully make you feel the emotions of these characters from Earth 1048. Brilliant and I can’t wait for the sequel! "



Areajugones


" I might be accused of being a bit of a fanboy, but I expect excellence from PlayStation exclusives, and for me, Marvel's Wolverine fell just short of that mark. It is a game that derives its strength from a powerful narrative, story, and cast of characters, backed by deeply satisfying combat, yet it doesn't offer much else. While it can be repetitive and occasionally feels a bit hollow, it ultimately delivers a spectacular final act and a story well worth experiencing. It doesn't quite capture the magic of Spider-Man 2 or the studio's other titles, but you’ll enjoy it if you appreciate narrative-driven games that steer clear of open-world formulas. Marvel's Wolverine 2—I await you with open arms. "



XGN


" An epic, brutal and visual stunning game that shines in its bossfights and where lineair progression works, but also where Insomniac doesn't seem to trust the intelligence of the gamer. "
