Death Stranding 2: On the Beach


About

Death Stranding 2: On the Beach is an action-adventure game developed by Sony Interactive Entertainment and released in 2025.


Death Stranding 2: On the Beach follows the story of Sam Porter Bridges as he embarks on a new expedition beyond the United Cities of America (UCA).
