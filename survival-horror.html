Top 5 Survival Horror Games




1. Amnesia: The Bunker

Amnesia: The Bunker is a survival horror game developed by Frictional Games and released in 2023.


Amnesia: The Bunker takes place in a WW1 French military bunker in 1916 where you play as Henri Clement, a French soldier who wakes up with severe amnesia.



2. Dead Space

Dead Space is a survival horror game developed by Motive Studio and released on 2023.


Dead Space takes place in the year 2508, when humanity is near-extinction and has spread throughout the universe.



3. The Outlast Trials

The Outlast Trials is a psychological survival horror game developed by Red Barrels and released in 2024.


The Outlast Trials is set in 1959 during the era of Cold War where you play as a 'Reagant', an involuntary human guinea pig abducted to test advanced methods of brainwashing, psychological conditioning, and mind control.



4. Resident Evil 2 (Remake)

Resident Evil 2 is a survival horror game developed by Capcom and released in 2019.


Resident Evil 2 takes place during a viral outbreak in Racoon City, caused by a leak of the T-virus, developed by Umbrella Corporation.



5. Silent Hill f

Silent Hill f is a survival horror game developed by NeoBards Entertainment and released in 2025.


Silent Hill f takes place in a rural japanese town of Ebisugaoka during the 1960s where you play as a highschool girl, Hanako Shimizu, who is trapped in a life of zero personal autonomy.
