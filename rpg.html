Top 5 RPGs




1. Bloodborne

Bloodborne is an action role-playing game developed by FromSoftware and released in 2015.


Bloodborne takes place in a dark, victorian era inspired city of Yharnam, whose inhabitants are afflicted with a blood-borne disease.



2. Diablo 4

Diablo 4 is an action role-playing game developed by Blizzard North and released in 2000.


Diablo 4 is the fourth main installment in the Diablo series, centering around Lilith, Mephisto's daughter, who has been summoned into Sanctuary.



3. The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim is an action role-playing game developed by Bethesda Game Studio and released in 2011.


The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim follows an unnamed prisoner, the Last Dragonborn, who discovers they have the soul and power of a dragon.



4. Persona 5 Royal

Persona 5 Royalis a role-playing game developed by P-Studio and released in 2016.


Persona 5 is a role-playing game where you play as a highschooler in Tokyo, Ren Amamiya.



5. Pokemon Legends: Z-A

Pokemon Legends: Z-A is an action role-playing game developed by Game Freak and released in 2025.


Pokemon Legends: Z-A takes place inside Lumiose city of Kalos Region, exclusively, and follows the events that took place 5 years ago in Pokemon X and Y.
